# ACS V1 prototype specification

## Objective

Demonstrate or reject damage-triggered passive hydraulic isolation in a redundant network.

## Geometry

- transparent planar body;
- at least two bypass routes around a central vulnerable branch;
- removable/swappable collar holder;
- dedicated leak port with repeatable opening geometry;
- visible working fluid;
- separate collection points for leak and useful outlet.

## Instrument interfaces

Design the body so that pressure taps, mass/flow collection and camera access can be added without changing the network topology.

## Isolation element

The swelling material is an **experimental variable**, not a fixed answer. Candidate materials must be characterized for response time, swelling ratio, confinement force, reversibility and fatigue. The model’s `tau` and final-conductance ratio are targets to measure, not properties to assume.

## Controls

A geometrically identical non-swelling collar is mandatory. A second control with no collar is recommended.
