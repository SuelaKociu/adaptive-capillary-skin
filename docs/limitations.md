# Limitations and claim boundaries

- No ACS physical prototype has been validated in this release.
- The hydrogel isolation law is phenomenological.
- Hagen–Poiseuille conductance assumes simplified rigid circular channels and steady laminar behavior.
- The network does not resolve multiphase priming, wall deformation, clogging, evaporation inside channels or transient inertia.
- The electrokinetic readout is a surrogate scaling model, not a calibrated sensor design.
- Atmospheric-water and cooling calculations are order-of-magnitude bounds.
- The refined Monte Carlo ensemble was derived after observing broad-ensemble failure and is therefore a design envelope, not independent validation.
- Every major ACS component class has relevant prior art. Novelty of the integrated architecture has not been legally established.
- Human–AI collaboration claims are currently a naturalistic case study plus a proposed prospective experiment; they are not yet causal evidence of synergy.
