# Research roadmap

## R0 — open computational record

Status: **complete in v0.1.0**.

## R1 — causal hydraulic prototype

Test damage → wetting → swelling → isolation → rerouting with matched negative controls.

## R2 — material characterization

Measure confined swelling kinetics, residual aperture, hysteresis, fatigue and contaminant sensitivity.

## R3 — sensing validation

Replace idealized voltage scaling with real electrodes, impedance characterization, drift analysis and blinded damage detection.

## R4 — environmental functions

Independently measure water capture and evaporative cooling. Do not infer these from hydraulic success.

## R5 — integrated skin

Combine functions only after each subsystem has standalone controls and uncertainty bounds.

## R6 — independent replication

Release complete raw data including failed devices and invite external laboratories to reproduce the mechanism.

## R7 — human–AI collaboration experiment

Run prospective blinded baseline conditions (human-only, AI-only, one-shot human+AI, iterative human↔AI) on unseen biomimetic design tasks.
