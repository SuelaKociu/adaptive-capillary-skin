# Prior-art scope and novelty discipline

ACS should not be described as “the first” or “never done before” without a dedicated professional search.

Known neighboring technology classes include:

- pump-free film-rupture/capillary sampling;
- hygromorphic actuators and pine-cone-inspired bilayers;
- hydrovoltaic / streaming-potential energy or sensing systems;
- fog/dew capture surfaces;
- vascular and microvascular self-healing materials;
- passive evaporative and radiative cooling systems.

The candidate differentiating mechanism to investigate is the **system-level coupling** in which the same working fluid participates in transport/state sensing and in damage-triggered local hydraulic isolation with redundant rerouting.

This document is a research-scoping statement only. It is not a patentability opinion, a validity analysis or freedom-to-operate advice.
