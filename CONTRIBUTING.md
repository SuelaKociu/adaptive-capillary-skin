# Contributing to Adaptive Capillary Skin

Contributions are welcome, especially **negative results**.

## High-value contributions

1. Independent physical replication of the damage-isolation mechanism.
2. Hydrogel swelling kinetics under channel confinement.
3. Capillary priming, evaporation and contamination measurements.
4. Electrokinetic readout with real electrodes and impedance characterization.
5. Prior-art corrections or closer technical precedents.
6. Model criticism, alternative constitutive laws and uncertainty analysis.
7. Replication of the human–AI collaboration study with blinded baselines.

## Evidence rules

Do not merge physical measurements into computational CSV files. Preserve raw untouched data, failed trials, calibration, specimen metadata, environmental conditions and protocol deviations.

## Claims

Use the labels **simulated**, **predicted**, **measured**, and **inferred** explicitly. A simulation result must never be relabeled as experimental evidence.
